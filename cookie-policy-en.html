Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and enhance your experience.

2. How we use cookies

Our website uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • To ensure the website functions properly
  • To remember your preferences and settings
  • To analyze how visitors use our website
  • To personalize content and advertisements

3. Types of cookies

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Necessary cookies Required for website functionality Session / 1 year
Analytics cookies Help understand how visitors use the site 1-2 years
Marketing cookies Used for personalized advertising 1 year

4. Your choices

When you first visit our website, you are given the option to accept or reject cookies. You can:

  • Accept all cookies - allows all cookies to operate
  • Reject all cookies - disables all cookies except necessary ones
  • Customize preferences - choose which cookies to allow

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the footer of our website.

5. Managing cookies

Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through settings. However, disabling cookies may affect some website features.

More information on managing cookies:

  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
